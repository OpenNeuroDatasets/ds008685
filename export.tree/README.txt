We present the Queensland Twin IMaging (QTIM) diffusion dataset: a diffusion neuroimaging dataset of young adult twins and siblings (18-30 years). This dataset comprises the 105 direction diffusion imaging data acquired on a subset of the QTIM participants. DSI Studio quality metrics are provided in the zip archive under derivatives in the file "group_dwi_qa.tsv".



Please cite: Shen, K. K., Rose, S., Fripp, J., McMahon, K. L., de Zubicaray, G. I., Martin, N. G., Thompson, P. M., Wright, M. J., & Salvado, O. (2014). Investigating brain connectivity heritability in a twin study using diffusion imaging data. NeuroImage, 100, 628–641. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2014.06.041



The other QTIM neuroimaging modality datasets are available at: https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds004169
